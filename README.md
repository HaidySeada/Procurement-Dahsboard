# Procurement & Sourcing Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing procurement and sourcing activities using Power BI to provide insights into purchase requisitions, purchase orders, supplier performance, purchasing costs, delivery timelines, and procurement operations.

The dashboard transforms procurement data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, KPI development, and interactive data visualization. It supports procurement teams and management in monitoring purchasing activities, identifying bottlenecks, evaluating supplier performance, and improving procurement efficiency.

## Objectives

- Monitor purchase requisitions (PRs) and purchase orders (POs) throughout the procurement cycle.
- Analyze procurement spending and purchasing trends over time.
- Evaluate supplier performance based on purchasing activity and delivery indicators.
- Track requisition-to-order progress and identify pending procurement activities.
- Monitor procurement lead times and purchasing cycle efficiency.
- Identify overdue purchase orders and potential delivery delays.
- Analyze buyer workload and purchasing performance.
- Improve procurement visibility, reporting accuracy, and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Gather procurement data related to purchase requisitions, purchase orders, suppliers, buyers, and purchasing activities.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values, duplicates, inconsistent supplier names, and incorrect data formats.
3. **Data Transformation:** Use Power Query to prepare and standardize procurement records for analysis.
4. **Data Modeling:** Establish relationships between procurement tables to enable accurate and consistent reporting.
5. **KPI Development:** Create DAX measures to monitor purchasing volumes, order status, spending, and procurement performance.
6. **Procurement Analysis:** Examine requisition and purchase order activity across suppliers, buyers, and business units.
7. **Supplier Performance Analysis:** Evaluate supplier activity and available delivery or fulfillment indicators.
8. **Trend Analysis:** Investigate purchasing patterns and procurement activity over time.
9. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive reports with dynamic filters and visualizations.
10. **Insights & Recommendations:** Identify opportunities to reduce delays, improve supplier coordination, and optimize procurement processes.

## Key Business Recommendations

- **Procurement Cycle Optimization:** Identify bottlenecks between requisition approval and purchase order creation to improve purchasing turnaround time.
- **Supplier Performance Improvement:** Monitor supplier delivery performance and purchasing trends to support supplier evaluation and sourcing decisions.
- **Purchase Order Follow-Up:** Establish consistent monitoring of pending and overdue orders to reduce the risk of operational delays.
- **Buyer Performance Monitoring:** Analyze workload distribution and purchasing activity to identify opportunities for process improvement.
- **Data Quality Governance:** Standardize supplier, buyer, and purchasing records to improve reporting accuracy.
- **Strategic Sourcing:** Use historical purchasing data to identify spending patterns and support supplier negotiations and sourcing strategies.
